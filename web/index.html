How does connecting work?

Sign in to Dropbox and allow doitdoit to access your task file.

Your tasks stay in Dropbox. This browser saves its connection credentials on this device and uses them to sync with Dropbox.

Changes appear in your terminal app and here on the next sync. If edits clash, we tell you and offer a recovery copy.
